JagX builds the products: multi-agent AI, secure social infrastructure, operating environments, backends and developer tools. From Lagos, it has shipped real products people use today — JagX AI and JagX Buddy Connect — with eleven product lines in motion.

JRILICENSE

Licensing · Integrity · Standards

JRILICENSE is the formal expression of licensing, integrity and standards around the growing family of products. Every product that ships carries the JRI mark — a guarantee of authenticity, build quality, security and global standard across digital and physical goods.
